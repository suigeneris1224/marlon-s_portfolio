Pairnest

A couple-focused shared budgeting and expense-tracking app designed to help partners coordinate spending and work toward shared financial goals.

Role Independent Full-Stack DeveloperStatus In developmentHosting OCI planned

Overview

Money planning
as a shared effort.

Pairnest is an independently developed mobile application for couples who want to plan budgets, track shared expenses, and work toward common financial goals. The product direction centers on making collaborative financial routines easier to manage together.

The application is currently in development. The screenshots show product work in progress and do not imply that the app is publicly released.

My role

Independent mobile
product development.

I am independently developing the application and its supporting backend, covering product design and implementation across the mobile client and server-side systems.

  • Mobile application UI/UX design and Flutter development.
  • Laravel backend development and application logic.
  • REST API architecture and integration between the app and backend.
  • Database development and application architecture.
  • Product workflows for shared budgets, expense tracking, and financial goals.

Product direction

Shared tools for
everyday finances.

Shared budgets

Plan spending together and see how shared budgets are progressing.

Expense tracking

Financial goals

Collaborative experience

Design the app around couples coordinating financial habits as a team.
