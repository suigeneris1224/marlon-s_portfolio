Overview

Customer engagement
operations in one system.

The Captive Portal CRM is an internal company application for Metro Retail. It is designed to help manage customer data associated with captive portal engagement and support marketing operations.

The project includes a Vue.js frontend and Laravel backend, with Retainful integration supporting customer segmentation and marketing campaign workflows. It is deployed on Metro Retail's internal network for internal users and is not publicly accessible.

My role

Application development
within a company team.

I contribute as a software developer on this internal company project. The work focuses on implementing the web application using the selected frontend and backend technologies and supporting the CRM’s customer engagement purpose.

  • Developing the Vue.js frontend and user-facing application workflows.
  • Developing backend functionality with Laravel and PHP.
  • Supporting the integration with Retainful for segmentation and marketing campaign operations.
  • Collaborating within the company project team on deployment and ongoing improvements.

The application is deployed for internal use only. No customer counts, internal URLs, or usage metrics are disclosed.
