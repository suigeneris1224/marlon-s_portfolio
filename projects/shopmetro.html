Overview

One commerce ecosystem.
Multiple retail experiences.

ShopMetro is an enterprise retail e-commerce ecosystem serving multiple business areas through a shared WordPress Multisite and WooCommerce platform. It brings online storefronts together with store-specific commerce rules, catalog operations, inventory feeds, and external services.

My professional work focuses on extending and operating the platform: building custom WooCommerce functionality, maintaining production behavior, automating operational tasks, and supporting the integrations that keep online and retail systems in sync.

My role

Engineering across
the commerce lifecycle.

I contribute as part of the professional engineering and operations team. My responsibilities include implementation, integration, deployment support, and ongoing maintenance of the corporate platform.

  • Developed custom WooCommerce plugins and store-specific features, including checkout, account, navigation, coupon, and product-pricing behavior.
  • Built and maintained automation for inventory synchronization and scheduled retail data workflows.
  • Supported integrations with RMS, FoodPanda, Grab, SFTP-based processes, and Retainful.
  • Implemented SEO improvements for the commerce platform.
  • Deployed and managed ShopMetro mobile application releases on Google Play and the Apple App Store. My responsibility was app publishing and release management, not development of the mobile application.
  • Maintained and optimized production applications and supported migration and operation in Oracle Cloud Infrastructure environments.
